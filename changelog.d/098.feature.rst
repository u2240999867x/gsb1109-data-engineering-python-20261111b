Added :func:`dateutil.parser.parse_consistent` for parsing a local wall time
against a list of candidate sites. Each site is checked with the time zone
rules it actually applies on that date, and every UTC instant that agrees
with the supplied abbreviation and numeric offset is retained. Fall-back
times can produce two candidates for one site, while spring-forward gaps can
produce none; sites that share one instant are stored with the site left
pending, and sites that disagree are reported for review. The result records
which of the original evidence pieces are load-bearing and which were missing,
without falling back to the host machine's local time zone.
